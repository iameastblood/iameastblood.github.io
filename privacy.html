Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

DogukanOS Backup is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to upload, list, download and delete the owner's own encrypted backup files.

  • Data is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload; Google user data is not read for any other purpose.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
